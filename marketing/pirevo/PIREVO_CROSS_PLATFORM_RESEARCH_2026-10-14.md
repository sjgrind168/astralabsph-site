## Selection gate
1. Identify category and evidence date across marketplaces.
2. Check current PIREVO slug, ASIN and parent product for duplicates.
3. Verify exact Amazon title, brand, count/size/color, customer-rating snapshot and review volume.
4. Assess review quality and major buyer risks, whether regulated/unsafe claims apply.
5. Use exact-variant product imagery and descriptive original buyer guide content. Never invent live prices/discounts/commissions.
6. Affiliate check: link resolves to exact item ASIN and includes permitted tag `pirevo-20`, with proper disclosure.
7. Commit first-party page and catalog; confirm deployment and image before Pin manifest `READY_FOR_PINTEREST`.
8. Only then produce premium full-height 2:3 distinct creative and publish through verified Pinterest action, not a guessed published state.
