## 3. Amazon Associates economics
Official category rates (rates can vary/change; verify before publishing):
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ
- Beauty (non-luxury), home, furniture, pets and many others 3%; handbags/accessories often 4%; Kitchen 4.5%; Luxury Beauty 10%.
- Use exact Amazon category assignment, not marketing description.
- Commission attributed to qualifying purchases under Amazon reporting rules; shipped items, returns, chargebacks matter.
- Standard referral session lasts up to 24 hours, with specific cart exception.
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G9SMD8TQHFJ7728F
Example **illustrative assumptions, NOT actual PIREVO ratios**: 1,000 PIREVO visitors, 30% outbound to Amazon, 5% of 300 Amazon clicks leading to qualified orders, $60 average qualifying basket, 4% category commission → 15 orders and **$36 commissions**. Achieving $1,000/mo at these hypothetical economics would require roughly 27,800 PIREVO visits, not a handful of Pins. We must measure real values.

## 4. Why organic Pinterest affiliate winners outperform
- Intent-led search phrases, not only product names: "best work satchel with pockets", "small travel toiletry bag organizer", "cozy Halloween mantle ideas", "kitchen chopper cleanup comparison".
- Cluster content by shopper problem and season. Fresh original 2:3 creatives, accurate exact-product visuals, separate board themes and useful linked pages.
- Link direct to relevant content, not generic homepage; prove shopper value before retailer CTA.
- Launch holiday inspiration early, since planning often happens weeks ahead; US 2026 holiday planning guidance: https://business.pinterest.com/blog/holiday-2026-starts-now-a-pinterest-guide-to-success/
- Maintain correct affiliate disclosures on Pin and PIREVO website.
- Prioritize purchase-ready queries and actual Amazon commissions; Pinterest impressions and saves are leading indicators, not revenue.

## 6. Revised 30-day PIREVO operating sequence
**Days 1–3: Audit, not volume.** Sign in to actual Pinterest Business analytics, confirm business-account visibility, claimed domain, image and board quality, board-specific title keywords, outbound click metric, UTM link collection and account warnings. Audit Buffer's 50 posts / 54 impressions discrepancy. Keep queue intact; avoid repeating identical creatives.

**Days 4–10: Controlled tests.** Publish one fully original, rights-cleared shopping Pin/day using 2–3 related themes (work satchels, beauty and kitchen utility). Unique keyword intent per Pin, on-topic board and exact PIREVO destination. Verify actual Pin IDs. Use Pinterest Trends region United States and compare queries.

**Days 11–20: Expand winning clusters.** Two carefully distinct creatives/day only if measured impressions and qualified clicks show reach. Add buyer-guide/supporting content (comparisons, sizing, safe usage, seasonal relevance). Test static product hero vs annotated practical comparison. Do not treat a single Amazon click as product winner.

**Days 21–30: Monetize proof.** Measure Pinterest outbound clicks, PIREVO sessions, product views and PIREVO Amazon clicks by SKU. Import actual Amazon Associates ordered/shipped items and commissions (with appropriate source/period caveats), then prioritize the best attributable product intent. Favor higher order values only when buyer needs, useful price/quality and commission categories justify them.

## 7. Simple performance scorecard
- Pinterest: created Pins, impressions, saves, outbound clicks, outbound rate per source/board/SKU; avoid confusing Pin clicks with outbound clicks.
- First-party PIREVO: unique campaign sessions, product-detail views, Amazon outbound events, product view rate, Amazon click/view rate.
- Retailer: Amazon Associates clicks, ordered items, shipped items, qualified sales, commissions, refunds if reported. Attribution may be aggregate, not 1:1 with a Pinterest user.
- Economics: earnings per qualified Amazon click, earnings per Pinterest referred session (only if source-level Amazon reports support attribution), refund-adjusted net commissions.
- Decisions: if impressions are near zero → account/distribution audit. If Pinterest clicks but site arrivals low → link/track/landing load. If site sessions but product views low → placement and navigation. If product views but Amazon clicks low → comparison and CTA. If Amazon clicks but no verified orders → buyer intent, product fit, category economics, merchant details, patience and sample size.
