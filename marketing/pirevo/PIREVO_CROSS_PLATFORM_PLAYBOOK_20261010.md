## Original assets and cross-platform treatment
- Pinterest: 2:3 static vertical new Pin with direct, UTM-tagged PIREVO product URL.
- Facebook: 4:5 product comparison carousel or video with linked PIREVO product page, explicit affiliate disclosure.
- Instagram: 4:5 carousel/reel with authentic original product visuals, CTA to PIREVO link in bio (regular Instagram caption URL typically isn't clickable).
- Threads: concise editorial text with PIREVO link; fresh angle, avoid duplicating Pins word-for-word.
- TikTok: original 9:16 23–60fps (video) or supported photo format, US-oriented English description, no false claim of US registration or eligibility for TikTok Shop US.
- YouTube Shorts: 9:16 meaningful original video with product details and an accurate description linking to PIREVO, don't simply animate a watermark-covered Pin.

## Campaign controls
1. Verify source creatives exist and can be repurposed legally. Draft CSV media not produced means posting is blocked until finished.
2. Check live queue limits before scheduling; TikTok's Buffer queue was at Free cap in Oct 10 audit.
3. No fake scarcity/discount, no unverified current Amazon price, claims of buying/trying product or misleading "viral" labels.
4. Confirm account identity, format and schedule; create platform-specific, unique captions.
5. Preserve UTM source per platform; measure actual landing visitors, product views, Amazon outbounds, independently verified orders/commissions.
6. **No ClickBank** anywhere in PIREVO project campaigns.

## TikTok US-targeting, legitimate path
