# PIREVO Pinterest Sales Studio — Production Standard v2.0
Updated: 2026-10-14 Asia/Manila
Owner: AstraLabs PH / PIREVO

## Objective
Generate qualified organic Pinterest traffic to first-party PIREVO product pages; then genuine Amazon Associate clicks and verified commissions. No fake clicks, sales or analytics. Zero-cost first.

## New volume target (owner override)
**10–15 distinct original premium Pins/day** across the whole campaign, replacing the legacy 2–3/day cap from the October 10 handoff. A target, not permission to spam or to publish unverified listings. Spread output across the day and across relevant boards. Never replicate identical creatives, descriptions or links indiscriminately. Adjust if Pinterest warnings/reach indicate low quality or spam risk. Do not claim daily execution unless a live automation or actual verified posting exists.

## Premium creative design lock
- Full bleed portrait 2:3, recommended 1000x1500 or larger; luxury editorial / photorealistic product-in-use styling.
- Catchy benefit-led headline in readable typography, 4–10 words ideal, honest supporting subhead, small PIREVO brand treatment, subtle single CTA.
- High contrast, legibility at mobile thumbnail, clean margins; no generic colored rectangles replacing real composition.
- Real branded item design/specs must never be changed or made up. If rendered in illustrative use-case imagery, do not imply the illustration is an exact product photograph.
- Mark AI-modified when appropriate. Use original or properly permitted creative assets. Check render visually at 100% and thumbnail before posting.

## Research loop
- Refresh Amazon Best Sellers/Movers & Shakers, verified product listings, Pinterest Trends/search signals, and recent credible editorial gift guides. Separate seasonal buzz from current sales.
- Rank categories by buyer intent, seasonality, evidence of demand, product quality/rating depth, listing reliability, visual demonstration, and competition.
- Audit existing catalog before adding a duplicate. Revisit high-intent seasonal picks frequently, refresh research dates and stale claim/rank/price text.
- Validate ASIN and selected variant, seller/stock availability where accessible, rating/review credibility, feature claims and safety concerns; a published article alone does not pass.
- Never claim a product is cheapest, sale-priced, #1 or currently in stock without live validated evidence. No invented product reviews.

## Destination and affiliate integrity
Pinterest Pin -> unique matching first-party /pirevo/products/{slug}/ or relevant PIREVO guide -> matching Amazon /dp/{ASIN}?tag=pirevo-20 . Verify displayed product, ASIN, merchant link destination and tag before publish. Use unique UTM content token per creative. First party disclosure and Pin description should disclose affiliate relationship. Do not replace original landing page with homepage or direct Amazon Pin. Review Amazon operating agreement and Pinterest policy before any new tactic; tagging syntax alone does not confirm actual referral tracking or commissions.

## Direct publication procedure
1. Check Pinterest @astralabsph Created Pins and drafts, native queued/scheduled state and Buffer before composing. Never double-post.
2. Work on an isolated Pinterest tab; do not navigate/refresh other users' tabs, notably the KDP Print Previewer.
3. Upload approved asset from a permitted path on user's Mac. Cross-check exact creative rendered in Pinterest preview; black thumbnails/previews are a stop sign.
4. Set unique benefit-led title, useful short description and disclosure, exact PIREVO UTM destination, correct topical board and AI marking.
5. Publish directly through Pinterest; obtain live permalink and screenshot/visible result. Only count live permalink as PUBLISHED; update manifest accordingly.
6. Record date, source, asset, destination, ASIN, board, Pin ID/permalink, status and observed analytics; distinguish actual from previous snapshots.
7. Respect current Pinterest recommendation to avoid duplicates and have relevance between image/text/landing page.

## MARKET SCOPE OVERRIDE — October 14, 2026 (OWNER DIRECTIVE)
**ONLY United States (US) and Canada (CA) shoppers are the target audience of PIREVO product discovery, Pinterest search strategy and affiliate conversion.** US is primary because Amazon.com account/tag `pirevo-20`; Canada is a secondary buyer audience, conditional on verified listing availability and earning attribution. No Philippine, UK or generic global shopping-trend data may qualify a new product on its own. Cross-marketplace demand evidence must explicitly relate to US/CA buyer behavior and dates. Former Shopee PH anecdotes are historical out-of-scope signals, not future sourcing input.

Research sources and segmentation:
- US: Amazon.com Best Sellers and Movers & Shakers, TikTok Shop US, Pinterest Trends US, Walmart US, Target, Costco US, US retailer/editorial reports and social-commerce data explicitly tagged United States.
- Canada: Amazon.ca product validation where applicable, Pinterest Trends Canada, Walmart Canada, Canadian retail/holiday signals, and exact product availability for Canadian buyers; do not presume US bestseller ranks match Canadian preferences.
- Distinguish geography in the research ledger using a market field `US` or `CA`, date of observation, verifiable seller/source, target product ASIN and evidence strength. Default ranked products to US until there is independent CA evidence.
- Only US/CA seasonal events and climate needs count: US Thanksgiving (November), Canadian Thanksgiving (October), Halloween, Black Friday/Cyber Monday and Christmas; respect varying climates and shipping constraints.
- English is the default Pinterest/website copy, written naturally for US and Canadian buyers. No PH pricing, Lazada, Shopee PH rankings, or local Philippine buyer-intent assumptions in new campaigns.
