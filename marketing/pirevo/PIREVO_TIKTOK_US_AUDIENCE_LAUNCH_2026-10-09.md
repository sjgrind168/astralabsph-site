- Do not create a second channel or log in to someone else's account without explicit user authorization. Existing AstraLabs PH TikTok account may remain independent. Proposed new brand channel requires sign-up/login and unique authorized contact method.
- Start free. No paid TikTok Ads or auto-credit system authorized.

- Affiliate transparency: #ad or an appropriately clear disclosure when content includes affiliate promotions, plus the disclosure visible on PIREVO. Confirm platform and program policies before publishing.
- The Amazon.com customer destination is optimized for US shoppers, but actual availability, shipping and pricing depend on the retailer.

## Baseline production standard
- 9:16 1080 x 1920, strong original hook in first 2 seconds, legible native-style captions.
- 15-30 seconds for discovery tests; no content-farm mass reposting, no repetitive unedited Amazon photos.
- Strong but grounded CTA: "See the complete product guide on PIREVO" (profile link only when active).
- Preserve exact product photography or user rights; do not synthesize brand features not present on the listing.
- Suggested test windows in **US Eastern Time**: late morning and early evening; compare actual analytics before selecting a final schedule. Respect daylight-saving changes.
- Use a separately attributable UTM for each approved video: utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=us_launch&utm_content=<unique_video_id>.
- Build each short around the *PIREVO on-site product page* rather than directing everyone to a generic homepage.
