## Mission and nonnegotiable scoreboard
The priority is **attributed purchase orders, shipped items, and actual affiliate commission**, not raw clicks, impressions or vanity follower counts.

The full funnel:
1. Discovery: Pinterest native impressions, Google organic clicks, source-tagged visitors.
2. Consideration: PIREVO product detail sessions, top pages, guide engagement.
3. Buyer intent: PIREVO outbound Amazon clicks by product (not guaranteed Amazon orders).
4. Retailer conversion: **Amazon Associates ordered items and shipped items from Amazon's own reports**.
5. Outcome: **reported commissions, refunds/returns, revenue per 100 qualified sessions**. Distinguish gross fees from finalized payouts.

**October 9/10 baseline:** About 35 PIREVO sessions in rolling seven days, 5 product views, 1 outbound click. Last imported Amazon Associates month-to-date report through Oct 9: 6 account-wide clicks, **0 ordered items, 0 shipped items and $0.00 earnings**. Amazon account clicks and PIREVO click events are different scopes. Search Console imported snapshots absent; native Pinterest impressions not imported. Data is early and sparse.

## SEO growth checklist
- Keep the new **/pirevo/trends/** indexable, canonical, internally linked from homepage and sitemap. Its 46 products are grouped into five clear user-intent categories.
- Create independent editorial buying guides with original comparisons, dimensions/caveats and clear shopping criteria; not thin copied Amazon listing text.
- Target US long-tail intents: "work satchel pockets and crossbody bag guide", "candle warmer lamp safe size comparison", "how to pick travel toiletry organizer", "pantry jar sizes labels". Avoid claims of guaranteed SEO rank.
- Use fast images, accessible headings, structured breadcrumbs and appropriate product-description schema without falsely presenting PIREVO as merchant or promising current prices.
- Verify index coverage/query metrics in authenticated Google Search Console; no snapshot is currently imported. Recheck queries in 7–14 days.
- Add thematic internal links from guide → 2–4 product detail pages → related guide, no link loops or keyword stuffing.

## Optimization decision rules
- **Clicks from Pins but no detail views:** mismatch between creative promise, link destination and landing page speed.
- **Product views but no Amazon outbounds:** improve criteria, placement/trust/CTA; check product fit and price/variant.
- **Amazon outbounds but no orders after meaningful volume:** compare legitimate retailer pricing, shipping, quality, seasonality, timing, and whether attribution/report scope matches; don't invent checkout data.
- **Orders but low commissions:** compare actual category rate, cancellations and returned orders; grow honest higher-value buyer interest where commission economics justify.
- **High views but no attributed clicks:** improve creative hook and platform-native search/board relevance, not bought traffic.
- **Sparse events:** collect more observations before declaring a best product. Small sample does not establish conversion causality.

### Platform screening
1. **ClickBank is a real affiliate marketplace**, but no seller/offer is approved just by being listed there. Focus on Home & Garden, Crafts & Hobbies, educational materials, practical design resources. Avoid miracle medical, rapid-money and fear-driven offers. Seller and product page must pass compliance, transparency, refund quality, payout and region eligibility.
2. **PartnerStack** offers SaaS affiliate programs, but requires partner-network/individual program approval and genuine referral credentials. Suitable for digital planning, document/productivity and creator tools when aligned to the buyer use case.
3. **impact.com** can provide direct retailer/brand affiliate alternatives, but each program requires verified acceptance, licensing and deep-link support. Could diversify physical picks without turning PIREVO into a random offer directory.
4. No ClickBank/PartnerStack/impact product or unverified affiliate link has been activated. Avoid confusing paid offers with existing Amazon listings.
5. For ClickBank compare CVR, EPC, Gravity, refund terms and seller reputation; Gravity is not net profit. Refunds can claw back commissions.
6. Account registration, tax residence and payout setup must use real business information and be reviewed before monetizing.
