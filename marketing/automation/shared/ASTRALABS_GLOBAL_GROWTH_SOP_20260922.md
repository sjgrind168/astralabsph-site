## 1. Customer and offer definition
**Astramate buyer:** deck officer/cadet/seafarer who needs to inspect several linked cargo/hold volume/weight/stowage, simple draft/trim or compass calculation steps without hunting through scattered notes. Actual promise: relevant, user-input-based calculations and visible working in a portable, offline-first toolkit, subject to supported features and Free/Premium limits. Free entry → hands-on verified tool → optional one-time lifetime Premium if they value more tools/ad-free access; no hardcoded global PH peso price. Prioritize cargo volume/weight/stowage/hold utilization and draft/trim, then compass. For advanced-stability marketing, first check the actual supported calculator ID, inputs, model assumptions and outputs; do not sell it as approved stability compliance. Make 1 post per actual buyer problem, not a laundry list.

**Keepry buyer:** a busy adult who has documents scattered across gallery, PDFs, folders and separate calendar/task apps, forgets user-entered renewal dates, or organizes records for more than one person. Actual promise: local-first personal Vault for images/PDFs with searchable details, Validity reminders and Life Admin tasks; Plus expands limits and supported recurring reminders/People profiles. Free essentials → real-life habit or use-case → optional one-time Plus when capacity/features matter. Explicitly avoid claims of automatic government renewal, document validation, perfect notifications, cloud restore, unverified encryption or guaranteed security.

**Priority decision rule:** buyers and actions over impressions. When data is too sparse, label tests exploratory, not proof of ROI.

Week 1 baseline: compare **hook A vs hook B** for same persona, keep meaningful differences in scenes. Week 2: compare one CTA/landing-page variant at a time. Week 3: if enough observations, inspect Play Console country/UTM/search metrics and change store listing assets with a controlled experiment. Week 4: concentrate on content with qualifying installs/purchases; remove ineffective generic themes. Do not assert statistically significant improvement from low counts.
Attribution: unique UTM `utm_source`/medium/campaign/content on ROOT homepage by channel and creative; preserve those campaign tags when a visitor selects an app and clicks its official Play Store button, no personally identifiable visitor tracking without appropriate notice/consent. Store content provenance and analytics records privately where needed; do not publish customer data.
