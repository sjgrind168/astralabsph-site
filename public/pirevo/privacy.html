PRIVACY

Privacy

PIREVO is designed to work without creating a reader account. The site does not ask visitors to submit payment information or create a profile.

Analytics

Optional Google Analytics support is built into the site but remains disabled unless a measurement ID is configured. If analytics is enabled, it may collect standard web usage information such as page views, device/browser information and referral source according to Google's policies.

Outbound retailer links

When you click a link to a retailer such as Amazon.com, you leave PIREVO and the retailer's own privacy and cookie policies apply.

Affiliate tracking

If affiliate links are enabled, the retailer may use tracking parameters or cookies to attribute qualifying purchases. PIREVO does not receive your full retail order details.

Contact

PIREVO is operated as an AstraLabs PH project. Visit astralabsph.com for contact information.
