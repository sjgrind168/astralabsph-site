Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026. Updated: October 19, 2026. This describes shoku by AstraLabs PH for iPhone, including the free plan and optional Lifetime Premium. We will update this page if future app versions change the data practices.

Journal and profile data

shoku records foods, portions, calories, macros, manual activity, preferences, and optional age, height and weight on your device. We do not require a shoku account, operate a shoku journal-sync server, sell this data, or use it for advertising. Your journal may be included in your own iPhone backup depending on your device settings. Keep any exported backup files secure.

App demonstration data

During internal testing, product screenshots and App Review demonstrations may use fictional meals, portions and activities rather than personal health records. These examples are generated locally from food reference data and activity estimates. They do not represent verified user measurements, are not used for advertising personalization and are not uploaded to AstraLabs PH servers. Public app releases do not automatically populate journals with demonstration records. Internal test records are removed before production submission.

Photos and camera

When you choose a food photo or scan a barcode, the camera or photo library is used only for that action. Photo-based food suggestions and barcode detection use device capabilities. shoku does not upload meal images to an AstraLabs PH server. Images are not attached to journal entries by default. You must confirm the food and amount yourself; cameras cannot accurately determine a cup's contents or serving weight.

Packaged-food barcode lookup

The barcode itself is read on your device. When you request an online product lookup, shoku sends the barcode digits to the independent Open Food Facts service to retrieve name, ingredients or nutrition information where available. This network request also makes ordinary connection information such as your IP address visible to that provider. shoku may keep a small cache of looked-up products on your device for faster future use. Review Open Food Facts' terms and policies. Their community-submitted data may be outdated or incomplete; always check your physical food label.

Optional Apple Health

Apple Health is an optional Premium feature. With your permission, shoku can read supported steps, workouts, active energy, body weight and height from Apple Health to display summaries or update your local profile. shoku does not write to Apple Health, and does not upload Apple Health records to an AstraLabs PH server. You can revoke access in iPhone Health settings.

Optional Premium voice logging

If enabled, shoku may request Microphone and Speech Recognition permission only when you tap to dictate a meal. Its dedicated voice feature requests on-device recognition when supported. shoku does not store the audio recording or upload audio to an AstraLabs PH server; recognized text appears in your editable meal field and is not saved until you confirm logging. Apple iOS keyboard Dictation, if you use it separately, is governed by Apple's settings and privacy practices.

App Store purchases

Optional Lifetime Premium purchases and restorations are handled by Apple's App Store/StoreKit. Apple processes billing, account and transaction information under Apple's terms; shoku checks transaction entitlement to unlock Premium, without collecting your payment card details. shoku does not use an independent payment gateway for iPhone Premium.

Local security and controls

shoku supports an optional device PIN or biometric unlock. Its native PIN verifier is stored using iOS Keychain, but the journal itself is not presented as independently end-to-end encrypted. You can edit or delete journal entries inside shoku. Uninstalling shoku removes its local app journal data, subject to any iOS backups or exported files you may separately retain. If you lose locally stored data without a backup, AstraLabs PH generally cannot recover it.

Children, nutrition and changes

shoku is a personal food journal, not a medical service or a substitute for professional dietary advice. We do not knowingly operate an account service for children. We may update this policy as the app evolves, and show the effective date here.
