SHOKU · Calorie & Macro Tracker

Support

Need help with your food and activity journal? SHOKU is designed for quick, local-first logging on iPhone. Contact us at contact@astralabsph.com.

Food entries and portions

Type a food, choose an available matching item, and confirm its serving weight or drink volume. Tap an existing journal entry to correct quantities. All nutrient figures are estimates that depend on the chosen product and portion. Unknown foods can be logged manually.

Barcode scanner

Allow camera permission when prompted, focus on a supported UPC/EAN code, then verify the matched product and serving. Online product lookup needs a connection to Open Food Facts, whose community database does not cover every brand. If nothing matches, enter barcode digits or nutrition facts from your physical package label.

Premium Voice

With an active trial or Lifetime Premium, turn on Voice Logging in SHOKU Settings, select a supported language, and tap Speak a meal. If your iPhone does not support on-device speech recognition for that language, select an available language or type the meal instead. Permit microphone and speech access in iPhone Settings if you previously denied it. Review transcription before logging; SHOKU does not save the meal automatically.

3-day trial and Lifetime Premium

SHOKU has a free basic tracker and an optional one-time Lifetime Premium plan. The planned Philippines launch price is ₱299, subject to the current Apple App Store checkout amount when sales become available. No recurring subscription is required. A three-day Premium trial may be offered once per eligible device. Availability is displayed inside the app.

Restore Purchases

In SHOKU Settings, select Restore Apple purchase. You must be signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Apple's App Store handles payment verification and restoration; restoration requires network access. If it cannot restore, confirm your Apple Account and try again or contact us.

Privacy and Apple Health

Apple Health is optional and read-only, and permissions can be changed in Apple Health settings. SHOKU stores your journal on device; keep a safe copy if you use Premium export. For data practices, read our privacy policy.
