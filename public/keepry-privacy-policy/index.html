Keepry Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Keepry is developed and operated by AstraLabs PH / ASTRALABS SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT SERVICES. Keepry is a local-first personal admin and private vault application for organizing documents, reminders, life-admin items, people, expiries, renewals, and related personal records.

1. Local-first design

Keepry is designed so that the personal content you add to the app is primarily stored on your device. In the current version, AstraLabs PH does not operate a cloud account that stores the contents of your Vault, your uploaded document images or PDFs, your Life Admin notes, your People profiles, or your reminders.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use Keepry, locally stored information may include:

  • Vault documents, images, PDFs, document metadata, reference numbers, issue dates, expiry dates, renewal history, categories, favourites, and notes;
  • Life Admin tasks, reminders, recurrence settings, completion history, people profiles, and related notes;
  • PIN and app-lock settings, biometric preference, theme and app preferences;
  • locally cached entitlement, trial, advertising, region-eligibility, and purchase-return state; and
  • backup files you choose to create or export.

3. No AstraLabs account required

Keepry does not require you to create an AstraLabs account or sign in with an email address to use its core features. The app may communicate with online services when necessary for trial eligibility, entitlement status, advertising, purchase verification, security, or related app functionality.

4. PIN and biometric unlocking

Keepry can use a local PIN to gate access to the app. The PIN verifier is processed and stored locally on the device. If you enable biometric unlocking, authentication is handled by the device operating system using its enrolled fingerprint, face, iris, or other supported biometric method. Keepry and AstraLabs PH do not receive or store your biometric template.

5. Smart Capture and extraction

Keepry's current Smart Capture / Smart Extract feature uses local, on-device heuristics to suggest fields such as a title, category, date, or reference number. These suggestions are not silently committed and should be reviewed by the user before saving. The current Smart Extract feature does not send document contents to a hosted AI extraction service.

6. Device entitlement information

Keepry may derive a one-way SHA-256 device key from a platform-provided device identifier to manage Free, Trial, and Keepry Plus entitlement. The raw device identifier used to derive that key is not sent to the AstraLabs backend. Server-side entitlement records may include the hashed device key, trial status, entitlement state, purchase-verification status, and related timestamps.

7. Google Play purchases

On Android builds distributed through Google Play, Keepry Plus purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. AstraLabs PH does not receive or store your credit card number, bank account information, Google account password, or other Google payment credentials. To verify and restore purchases, Keepry may process purchase-verification information such as product identifiers, purchase tokens, order identifiers, acknowledgement state, purchase state, and verification timestamps through the AstraLabs backend and Google Play services.

8. Direct purchases outside Google Play

Where a non-Play or direct distribution build offers a direct Keepry purchase, checkout may be processed through PayMongo. AstraLabs PH does not receive or store your full card number or banking credentials. The backend may retain payment-reference information, checkout-session identifiers, payment status, event identifiers, and timestamps needed to verify entitlement and maintain payment records.

9. Advertising

The Free version of Keepry may display advertisements through Google advertising services such as AdMob. Where required, the app may request advertising consent before ads are displayed and may provide privacy options through the advertising platform. Trial and Keepry Plus access are intended to be ad-free. Third-party advertising services may process device, advertising, usage, diagnostic, consent, and network information according to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

10. Coarse country signal and MerchLab fallback

For eligible Free users in the Philippines, Keepry may display a MerchLab promotional fallback linking to external marketplaces when a Google ad is unavailable. On Android, Keepry may use only coarse cellular network and SIM country codes to determine Philippines eligibility. Keepry does not use GPS, precise location, locale, language, Advertising ID, or billing country for this eligibility check. A coarse PH / non-PH result may be cached locally on the device for a limited period.

11. External and affiliate links

Keepry may contain links to external websites, app stores, payment providers, Shopee, Lazada, or other third-party services. When you open an external link, you leave Keepry and become subject to the privacy policy and terms of the external service.

12. How information is used

Information processed by Keepry may be used to operate app functionality, manage Free / Trial / Plus access, verify and restore purchases, provide advertising where applicable, maintain security and reliability, prevent misuse or duplicate entitlement, diagnose technical issues, provide support, and improve the app.

13. Data sharing and service providers

AstraLabs PH does not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate Keepry, including Google Play distribution and billing, Google advertising services, Supabase backend infrastructure, PayMongo for eligible direct purchases, app-store services, security providers, hosting providers, and technical operations.

14. Data security

AstraLabs PH uses reasonable technical safeguards designed to protect information processed by Keepry. These include local app-lock controls, platform biometric authentication where enabled, one-way hashed device identifiers, HTTPS connections for backend requests, platform purchase verification, and backend access controls. No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should keep their device, operating system, PIN, and backups secure.

15. Data retention

Locally stored Keepry content remains on your device until you delete it, erase Keepry data, restore or replace data from a backup, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Server-side entitlement and purchase-verification records may be retained as reasonably necessary for entitlement integrity, fraud prevention, payment reconciliation, legal, accounting, security, or app-store requirements.

16. Data deletion requests

You can delete locally stored Keepry content from within the app or by clearing/uninstalling the app, subject to your device platform. You may request deletion of eligible server-side information associated with Keepry by contacting contact@astralabsph.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to locate the relevant entitlement or payment record without requesting your PIN or biometric information.

17. Backups

Keepry may allow you to create or export a backup. Backups are under your control once created. AstraLabs PH does not automatically receive your exported backup. You are responsible for storing backup files securely and for deciding where they are copied, shared, or restored.

18. Children's privacy

Keepry is a personal administration and document-management utility and is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. AstraLabs PH does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

19. Your responsibility for stored records

Keepry is an organizational tool and does not replace official records, professional advice, legal requirements, government systems, or the user's responsibility to protect sensitive documents and verify important dates, renewals, and information.

20. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Keepry features, service providers, regulatory requirements, or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
