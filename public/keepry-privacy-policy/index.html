Keepry Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: September 16, 2026

Keepry is developed and operated by ASTRALABS SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT SERVICES, trading as AstraLabs PH, in the Philippines. Keepry is a local-first personal admin and private vault application for organizing documents, reminders, life-admin items, people, expiries, renewals, and related personal records. This Privacy Policy explains what Keepry processes, what stays on your device, what may leave your device, why it is processed, who may receive it, how long it may be retained, and the choices and rights available to you.

1. Who controls information processed by Keepry

For personal data that AstraLabs PH receives through Keepry's backend, support channels, or direct payment operations, the responsible business is ASTRALABS SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT SERVICES / AstraLabs PH.

Privacy contact: AstraLabs PH

Website: https://www.astralabsph.com

Email: contact@astralabsph.com

Country: Philippines

2. Local-first design and sensitive content

The contents you place in Keepry are designed to remain primarily on your device. In the current version, AstraLabs PH does not operate a Keepry cloud account that automatically uploads or stores the contents of your Vault, your uploaded document images or PDFs, your Life Admin notes, your People profiles, or your reminders.

You may choose to store highly sensitive records in Keepry, including identity documents, legal records, financial records, certificates, vehicle records, property records, membership records, or information about other people. Unless a future feature clearly tells you otherwise and obtains any required disclosure or consent, that content is processed locally and is not sent to AstraLabs PH servers.

AstraLabs PH does not use the contents of your Keepry Vault to train AI models.

3. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use Keepry, information stored locally may include:

  • Vault documents, images, PDFs, titles, categories, reference numbers, issue dates, expiry dates, renewal history, favourites, and notes;
  • Life Admin tasks, due dates, status, recurrence settings, completion history, and notes;
  • People profiles you manually create inside Keepry;
  • reminders and local app preferences;
  • PIN verifier data, app-lock settings, and your biometric-unlock preference;
  • locally cached trial, entitlement, advertising-consent, coarse region-eligibility, and payment-return state; and
  • backup files you intentionally create or export.

5. No AstraLabs account required

Keepry does not require you to create an AstraLabs account or sign in with an email address to use its core features. A device-based entitlement system is used instead. Because no AstraLabs user account is created, there is no AstraLabs account-deletion flow. Local Keepry content can be erased from the app or by clearing/uninstalling the app, subject to your device platform.

6. PIN and biometric unlocking

Keepry can use a local PIN to gate access to the app. The PIN verifier is generated and stored locally. AstraLabs PH does not receive your PIN.

If you enable biometric unlocking, authentication is handled by your device operating system using its enrolled fingerprint, face, iris, or other supported biometric method. Keepry receives only the success/failure result needed to unlock the app. AstraLabs PH does not receive or store your biometric template.

App lock is an access-control feature. It does not replace full-device security, operating-system encryption, or secure handling of exported files.

7. Camera, file picker, and Smart Capture

If you choose to scan with the camera or select a file, Keepry uses the system camera/file handoff only for the content you intentionally select or capture. Keepry does not continuously record camera input.

Keepry's current Smart Capture / Smart Extract feature uses local, on-device heuristics to suggest fields such as a title, category, date, or reference number. Suggestions are not silently committed and should be reviewed before saving. The current feature does not send document contents to a hosted AI extraction service.

8. Device entitlement information

Keepry may derive a one-way SHA-256 device key from a platform-provided device identifier to manage Free, Trial, and Keepry Plus entitlement. The original device identifier used to derive that key is not sent to the AstraLabs backend. Server-side records may include the hashed device key, trial status, entitlement state, purchase-verification status, and timestamps.

9. Google Play purchases

On Android builds distributed through Google Play, Keepry Plus purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. AstraLabs PH does not receive or store your card number, bank credentials, Google password, or other Google payment credentials.

To verify and restore purchases, Keepry may send purchase-verification information, including a Google Play purchase token and product identifier, to the AstraLabs backend, which may verify the purchase with Google Play. Google may independently process account and payment information under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

10. Direct purchases outside Google Play

Certain direct or non-Google-Play distributions may offer checkout through PayMongo. This direct checkout is not used as a substitute for Google Play Billing inside the standard Google Play build unless a Google-approved program or applicable platform rule expressly permits it.

AstraLabs PH does not receive or store your full card number or banking credentials from PayMongo. AstraLabs may retain limited payment-reference information, checkout/session identifiers, payment status, event identifiers, and timestamps needed for entitlement, reconciliation, fraud prevention, support, accounting, and legal obligations.

11. Advertising and consent

The Free version may display advertising through Google AdMob. Trial and Keepry Plus are intended to be ad-free. Where required, Keepry uses Google's consent tools before requesting ads and may provide an advertising privacy-options control.

Depending on device settings, consent choices, region, and Google Mobile Ads configuration, Google advertising services may process information such as device or account identifiers (including advertising-related identifiers where available), IP/network information, app interactions, advertising interactions, consent status, and diagnostic information. AstraLabs PH does not receive your Vault content for advertising.

Advertising processing by Google and its partners is also governed by their own privacy terms and controls.

12. Coarse Philippines eligibility signal

For eligible Free users in the Philippines, Keepry may display a MerchLab promotional fallback linking to external marketplaces when a Google ad is unavailable. On Android, Keepry may use coarse cellular network and SIM country codes to determine Philippines eligibility. Keepry does not use GPS or precise location for this check. The coarse PH/non-PH result may be cached locally for a limited period.

The cellular/SIM country values used by this Keepry feature are processed locally and are not sent to the AstraLabs entitlement backend. Third-party advertising services may independently derive approximate location from network information under their own practices.

13. External and affiliate links

Keepry may contain links to external websites, app stores, payment providers, Shopee, Lazada, or other third-party services. When you open an external service, its own privacy policy, security practices, and terms apply. AstraLabs PH is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of independent third-party services.

14. Legal bases and purposes of processing

Depending on the feature and applicable law, AstraLabs PH processes limited backend or support data on one or more of the following bases:

  • to provide functionality or a transaction you requested, including trial, entitlement, purchase, and restoration services;
  • your consent, where consent is required, including certain advertising choices;
  • legitimate business interests permitted by law, such as securing the service, preventing fraud or duplicate entitlement, maintaining reliability, and responding to support issues; and
  • compliance with legal, tax, accounting, consumer-protection, platform, or regulatory obligations.

Keepry does not use your Vault content for unrelated marketing or profiling.

15. Automated processing

Keepry uses automated rules to apply Free, Trial, and Plus access, quotas, purchase verification, and feature availability based on stored entitlement or purchase state. These rules do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Google advertising systems may use automated systems for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, or personalization when permitted by your consent and settings. Those systems are governed by Google and applicable advertising-partner policies.

16. Service providers and recipients

Limited data may be processed by service providers needed to operate Keepry, including:

  • Google, for Google Play distribution, Play Billing, purchase verification, Play services, and AdMob advertising;
  • Supabase, for Keepry's backend entitlement and payment-verification infrastructure;
  • PayMongo, where an eligible direct/non-Play checkout is offered;
  • hosting, security, email, and technical service providers used by AstraLabs PH to operate its website and support channels; and
  • government authorities or other recipients when disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.

AstraLabs PH does not sell your personal information.

17. International processing

Keepry's current Supabase backend is hosted in Southeast Asia (Singapore). Google, PayMongo, advertising partners, hosting providers, or their subprocessors may process limited data in other countries where they or their service providers operate. When AstraLabs PH uses processors for personal data, we seek to use providers and contractual/technical safeguards appropriate to the processing and applicable law.

18. Data security

AstraLabs PH uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to Keepry's current architecture, including HTTPS for backend communications, one-way hashed device identifiers for entitlement, platform purchase verification, app-lock controls, device-provided biometric authentication where enabled, and restricted backend credentials/access.

No application, device, transmission, or storage system can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your device, PIN, operating-system account, and any copies or backups you create.

19. Backups and exported files

Keepry Plus may allow you to export a user-controlled backup containing your Keepry records and document files. Your Keepry PIN is not included in the exported archive.

Current Keepry export files are not independently encrypted by Keepry. Anyone who obtains an exported backup may be able to read its contents. Store exported backups only in locations you trust, protect them using device/storage encryption or another secure method, and delete copies you no longer need. AstraLabs PH does not automatically receive your exported backup.

20. Retention

  • Local content: remains on your device until you delete it, erase Keepry data, clear app data, replace it through a restore, or uninstall the app, subject to operating-system or device backup behavior outside Keepry's control.
  • Entitlement/trial records: may be retained while needed to enforce one-time trial rules, restore a valid lifetime entitlement, prevent abuse, or maintain service integrity.
  • Purchase/payment records: may be retained as needed for purchase restoration, reconciliation, fraud prevention, disputes, accounting, tax, legal claims, and platform or regulatory requirements.
  • Support/privacy communications: may be retained for the time reasonably necessary to resolve the request and maintain an appropriate business record.

When information is no longer reasonably required, AstraLabs PH may delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it, subject to legal or platform retention requirements.

21. Deletion and control

You can delete individual Keepry records from within the app and can use the app's erase function to remove Keepry content from the current device. Clearing app data or uninstalling may also remove locally stored content, subject to device/OS backup behavior.

Because AstraLabs PH does not receive the contents of your local Vault, it cannot retrieve or delete local records on your behalf.

You may request access to or deletion of eligible server-side records by emailing contact@astralabsph.com. We may request limited information reasonably necessary to locate and verify the relevant record. We will never ask for your Keepry PIN or biometric template. Some purchase, fraud-prevention, legal, accounting, or platform records may need to be retained even after a deletion request.

22. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and the nature of the data we actually hold, you may have rights to be informed, access personal data, request correction, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, request erasure or blocking where legally available, and obtain portability where applicable.

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, data subjects also have rights recognized by the National Privacy Commission. Requests can be sent to contact@astralabsph.com. If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may also contact or file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

23. Security incidents

If AstraLabs PH becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting data under its control, it will investigate and take reasonable containment and remediation steps. Where notification to affected users or regulators is required by applicable law, AstraLabs PH will provide notification in accordance with those requirements.

24. Children's privacy and intended audience

Keepry is intended as a personal administration and document-management utility for adults and is not directed to children under 13. AstraLabs PH does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that backend personal data of a child was collected contrary to applicable requirements, we will take reasonable steps to delete or otherwise address it.

25. Changes to Keepry or this policy

Keepry's features and service providers may evolve. If a future feature changes how personal or sensitive data is collected, used, or shared, AstraLabs PH will update this policy and, where required, provide an in-app disclosure or obtain consent before the new processing begins.

The latest policy will be published at this URL. Material changes may also be communicated through the app, store listing, or another reasonable channel.
