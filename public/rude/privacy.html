RUDE · Local-first personal finance

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026. This policy covers RUDE for iPhone by AstraLabs PH. We may update the effective date and details when RUDE's capabilities or data practices change.

Personal financial records

RUDE lets you manually record financial accounts, balances, ledger transactions, debt schedules and payments, budgets, bills, saving goals, investments and optional financial priorities or coach questions. The consumer finance profile stays in the app's local protected storage; RUDE does not require a consumer account or automatically send this personal ledger to an AstraLabs PH server. Estimates use your recorded amounts and any exchange rates you enter, not direct bank access, verified lender statements or live pricing.

What leaves your device

Your personal finance records are not automatically uploaded by RUDE. If you deliberately export a CSV, share a passphrase-protected encrypted RUDE backup, or import a file using iOS file controls, the copies you choose to share or store may be processed by other apps, cloud drives or services you select. CSV is plaintext and should be handled with care. Finance backup files use passphrase-based encryption, but do not rely on an export you have not saved somewhere safe.

Optional owner-only AstraLabs earnings window

RUDE includes a separately authenticated reporting window intended for an AstraLabs PH business owner. When that window is used and connected, RUDE communicates over HTTPS with AstraLabs PH's business-reporting service to authorize the owner and retrieve business metrics. Its session token is stored in the iPhone Keychain. This reporting connection does not send your personal RUDE accounts, debts or transaction ledger. As with other web connections, the reporting service may receive normal request metadata such as an IP address and request time. This business-reporting feature is distinct from RUDE's offline consumer finance tools.

Permissions and device security

RUDE may ask you to use device Face ID or passcode to protect access to your finances or the separate owner reporting window. Authentication is handled by iOS. Optional local reminder notifications require iOS permission and schedule due-date reminders on your device without a server. RUDE is not an account aggregator and does not ask for consumer online-banking passwords or payment-card credentials.

Advertising, tracking and analytics

RUDE has no advertising SDK in its finance feature and does not use your personal finance records for targeted advertising. The separate owner reporting connection is not a consumer finance analytics upload. Apple and device services may handle diagnostics under your iOS privacy settings. We will revise these disclosures if the app's analytics, SDKs or practices change.

Your privacy controls and deletion

  • Review, edit or remove individual financial records in RUDE.
  • Clear RUDE's locally saved coach conversation history from the Financial Priority screen.
  • Create an encrypted backup or export a plaintext CSV from Settings → Data control.
  • Use Settings → Data control → Erase all RUDE finances and type ERASE to permanently replace the financial profile on that device with a blank one and cancel its due reminders.

Erasing the local financial profile does not erase files you previously shared or saved elsewhere, system device backups, or a separately authorized owner reporting connection. Removing RUDE from the device ordinarily removes its local app container, subject to your separate device backup retention settings. We generally cannot recover finance files never shared with us.

Important limitations

RUDE's money-health score, debt payoff comparisons, spending forecasts and suggested next steps are estimates for personal recordkeeping. They are not individualized regulated investment advice, credit reporting, bank statements, a lending product, or guarantees of savings. Verify financial decisions independently.
