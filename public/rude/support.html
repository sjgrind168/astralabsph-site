RUDE Support

RUDE is an offline-first personal finance tracker for iPhone. Your personal financial data is stored on your device rather than in an online RUDE account.

How do I back up my finances?

Open Settings → Encrypted backup / restore / CSV tools, set a passphrase with at least 12 characters, create an encrypted .rudebak file, then save it with iOS Share. Keep the passphrase and the file safe. A plaintext CSV is not an encrypted backup.

How do I erase local records?

Open Settings → Encrypted backup / restore / CSV tools → Erase all RUDE finances on this iPhone. Confirm by typing ERASE. This is irreversible without a separate backup. Files you exported elsewhere are not erased by the app.

What if an import fails?

Confirm the file format and account mapping. RUDE supports native RUDE transaction CSV and reviewed compatible bank CSV or .xlsx statements. Imports are validated before saving. Keep the original statement available for reconciliation.

What does RUDE's Financial Brain use?

Rules based on your logged transactions, plans, chosen reserve, loan rates and manually entered currency rates. It does not connect to consumer bank accounts or guarantee future income.

Contact AstraLabs PH

For support or privacy questions, email contact@astralabsph.com. Include your app version, iOS version and a non-sensitive description of the problem. Never email account numbers, bank passwords, full financial statements or backup passphrases.
