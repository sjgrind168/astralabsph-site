Frequently asked questions

Does Keepry require an account?

No AstraLabs sign-in is required for Keepry’s core features.

Is Keepry free?

Yes. Keepry Free includes limits on Vault documents, Life Admin items, reminders and People profiles. Plus expands allowances and removes advertising.

Does my data disappear when the trial ends?

Keepry does not automatically delete data created over Free limits; excess items can become temporarily unavailable under the Free allowance unless you reduce usage or upgrade.

Keepry primarily stores your personal records on your device. Make and safely store backups if you need to restore them after losing or replacing your device.
